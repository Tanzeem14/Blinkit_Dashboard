# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, product categories, outlet characteristics, fat content, and outlet-level performance.

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard** provides a comprehensive analysis of sales data to understand overall business performance, product-level sales, outlet performance, and customer-oriented product characteristics.

The dashboard enables users to explore sales trends and compare performance across different item types, outlet sizes, outlet tiers, fat content, and establishment years.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Track key business KPIs.
- Compare sales across different item types.
- Analyze sales based on fat content.
- Evaluate outlet performance by tier and size.
- Identify sales trends based on outlet establishment year.
- Provide an interactive interface for business analysis.

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

- **Total Sales:** $1.20M
- **Average Sales:** $140.99
- **Total Items:** 9K
- **Average Rating:** 3.92

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Outlet Establishment Trend

A line chart showing **Total Sales by Outlet Establishment Year**, helping identify changes in sales performance over time.

### 2. Outlet Size Analysis

A donut chart showing the distribution of outlets based on outlet size:

- Medium
- Small
- High

### 3. Fat Content Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales based on:

- Low Fat
- Regular

It also provides a comparison of fat-content sales across different outlet tiers.

### 4. Sales by Item Type

A horizontal bar chart showing sales performance across different product categories, including:

- Fruits & Vegetables
- Snack Foods
- Household
- Frozen Foods
- Dairy
- Canned
- Baking Goods
- Health & Hygiene
- Meat
- Soft Drinks
- Breads
- Hard Drinks
- Others
- Starchy Foods

### 5. Outlet Tier Analysis

Sales are compared across:

- Tier 1
- Tier 2
- Tier 3

This helps identify which outlet location tier contributes the most to overall sales.

## 🔎 Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

- **Item Type**
- **Item Identifier**

Users can select specific products or item types to dynamically analyze their sales performance across the dashboard.

1. Download or clone this repository.

3. Use the available slicers to filter the dashboard.
4. Interact with the charts to analyze sales performance across different dimensions.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Total sales generated are approximately **$1.20M**.
- The average sales value is approximately **$140.99**.
- The average product rating is **3.92**.
- **Tier 3 outlets** contribute the highest sales among the three outlet tiers.
- Regular-fat products contribute a larger share of sales compared to low-fat products.
- Medium-sized outlets represent the largest portion of the outlet distribution.
- **Fruits & Vegetables** and **Snack Foods** are among the highest-performing item categories.
- Sales vary across outlet establishment years, with the highest observed sales reaching approximately **$205K**.
